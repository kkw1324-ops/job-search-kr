# 3대3 대결 (지원자 측 대 사측)

The people applying argue against the people selecting, over one draft. Run it when the user asks or for a high-priority posting, after the ⑦ reflection and before the ⑧ re-judgment (procedure: [review.md](../review.md) 「3대3 대결」). Shared definitions, including the meaning of 통과 / 보류 / 탈락 and the three groups of results: [공통.md](공통.md) 「판정 구분」, 「결과의 세 분류」.

## Teams

Employer side (사측): three hiring managers of the applying company. All three judge by `panel/인사.md`, the posting, and the company analysis. They look for reasons not to pass the applicant.

| Role name | Looks at | 인사.md items |
|---|---|---|
| 사측 서류 심사 | Rejection reasons, posting requirements, job relevance | 1, 2, 3, 16 |
| 사측 회사 적합 | Why this company and job, organizational fit, consistency with the career-change story and common axis, overall impression, reason left to keep this applicant | 6, 13, 15, 17, 18, 19 |
| 사측 진정성 | Exaggeration, impression-management sentences, reads as templated or AI-written, whether the applicant could defend it in an interview (1-minute explanation test) | 4, 5, 9, 12, 20 |

In rounds 1 and 2, where the roles raise issues, each 사측 role raises issues only from the 인사.md items in its row; in Round 3 every agent rules on every issue, whatever its row. A 사측 role's 통과 / 보류 / 탈락 for a 칸 is a verdict on those items, named with the words of 공통.md 「판정 구분」; it is not the H of 인사.md item 15. The four per-칸 「뽑고 싶은가」 conditions of item 15 rest on items 16 (①), 17 (②) and 3 (③), which no single 사측 role covers (3 and 16 are 사측 서류 심사's; 15 and 17 are 사측 회사 적합's), so no role judges them alone: 사측 회사 적합 uses item 15 only for the reason left to keep the applicant (② and ④), and the 칸's 「뽑고 싶은가」 in a bout is the three Round 3 verdicts taken together under 「Result」.

Applicant side (지원자 측): the applicant and the applicant's support team of experts.

| Role name | Argues from | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 지원자 본인 | `profile/profile.md`, the cards in `profile/stories/` (「⑤ 사용자의 말」, 「⑦ 경계」), `profile/source/` | Says what actually happened and what the applicant meant |
| 지원자 측 직무 전문가 | The 직무 프로필, `panel/직무.md` | Shows how the experience bears on the applied job, within the facts |
| 지원자 측 채용 서류 전문가 | `panel/서류.md`, `writing-common.md`, `essay-kr.md` (sections per `job-reviewer.md` 「읽을 것」) | Defends the wording or proposes the repair |

## Rounds

Every agent is a fresh `job-reviewer` with one role. Each round's agents run in parallel.

1. **공격**: the three 사측 roles read the draft independently and raise issues.
2. **변론**: the main conversation numbers and merges the issues, then gives the list to the three 지원자 측 roles.
3. **판정**: three new agents, one per 사측 role, read the draft, the issue list, and the defenses, and rule on every issue.

Two drafts for the same company may share one bout. A 칸 whose text is identical in both drafts is debated once.

## Rules for both sides

- Quote sentences that are in the draft, verbatim.
- 사측 does not demand facts as if the applicant should invent them. If only a fact missing from the profile would resolve an issue, say so.
- 지원자 측 argues only from the records. No facts, numbers, feelings, or wider scope absent from the profile. A proposed sentence follows 「금지 표현」 and 「AI 문체 신호」 in `writing-common.md` and the card's 「⑦ 경계」.
- 사측 judges only the written text, not what the applicant could add later.
- A defense that shows the text already answers the issue wins. A defense that only says the issue is unfair does not.
- Every issue is sorted into one of the three groups of 「결과의 세 분류」: 고치면 풀리는 것 (the profile or card already holds the fact, so rewriting the 칸 resolves it), 물으면 풀리는 것 (only a question that falls under 공통.md 「G에 내는 질문」, or a 「확인 필요」 fact), 글로 풀리지 않는 격차 (no sentence can resolve it). Missing material that 공통.md sends to E as 「재료 없음」 is not 물으면 풀리는 것: sort that issue by what remains without the fact (고치면 풀리는 것 if removing or rewording the claim resolves it, otherwise 글로 풀리지 않는 격차) and write 「재료 없음」 in the 이유 column.

## Return format

Write the role name on the first line. Do not count characters or edit files.

**Round 1 (사측)**
| 칸 | 문장(초안 그대로) | 쟁점 | 근거(인사.md 항목, 공고, 기업 분석) |

Then for each 칸: 통과 / 보류 / 탈락 (meanings in 공통.md 「판정 구분」), with one line of reason.

**Round 2 (지원자 측)**
| 쟁점 번호 | 인정 / 반박 / 재료 없음 | 근거(파일과 문장) | 바꿀 문장(인정일 때) |

**Round 3 (사측)**
| 쟁점 번호 | 유지 / 철회 | 분류(고치면 풀리는 것 / 물으면 풀리는 것 / 글로 풀리지 않는 격차) | 이유 |

Then for each 칸: 통과 / 보류 / 탈락, with the sentence that decided it.

## Result

- An issue stands if two or more of the three Round 3 agents keep it; otherwise 지원자 측 wins it.
- A 칸 is 통과 only if two or more of the three Round 3 agents say 통과.
- A 칸 that is not 통과 is 보류 or 탈락 as the majority of the three Round 3 verdicts say, with the meanings in `공통.md` 「판정 구분」. An item-16 rejection reason inside the 칸 that is left unresolved after Round 3 decides the 칸 as 탈락 whatever the vote (인사.md 「거르기」: a hit is not raised by good scores on other items).
- Standing issues are applied by the main conversation under 「반영」 in `review.md`. An issue in 물으면 풀리는 것 becomes a G question (`panel/공통.md` 「G에 내는 질문」) or a 「확인 필요」 check; an issue marked 「재료 없음」 in Round 3 is written in E as 「재료 없음」 and is not asked; one in 글로 풀리지 않는 격차 becomes 「남은 위험」 or goes to interview preparation.
- The 칸 verdicts here are given before the ⑧ re-judgment. Report them as such; the changed 칸 are judged again after the fixes (`review.md` 「⑧ 재심사와 마감」).
- The result judges document quality, not the chance of passing. No score is given.
